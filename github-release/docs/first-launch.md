# First Launch Guide — IDeV Advanced Pro

Welcome to IDeV Advanced Pro! Follow these simple steps after installation to get started.

---

## 1. Prerequisites Check
IDeV Advanced Pro connects to your local Ollama engine for executing open-weight model weights:
- Ensure **Ollama** is running on your machine.
- You can verify this by visiting `http://127.0.0.1:11434` in your browser (it should display `"Ollama is running"`).

---

## 2. Launching the Workspace
1. Launch **IDeV Advanced Pro** from your desktop shortcut or Windows Start Menu.
2. The application will initialize its internal proxy and catalog engine within 1–2 seconds.
3. You will be greeted by the clean conversation workspace.

---

## 3. Selecting a Model
Click the model selector pill in the top bar:
- **Cloud Models (Top Section)**: Pinned with `☁️` icons (`deepseek-v4.1-flash:cloud`, `gemma4:31b-cloud`, `glm-5.3-flash:cloud`, `kimi-k3:cloud`, etc.). Requires an active internet connection.
- **Local Models (Main Section)**: Over 1,100 specific size and parameter variants across 244 open-weight model families.
- Select your preferred model to activate it immediately.

---

## 4. Attaching Images & Documents
- **Clipboard Screenshots**: Simply press `Ctrl+V` in the chat input to paste screenshots from your clipboard.
- **File Attachments**: Click the `+` button in the message composer to open the native Windows file selection dialog.
- The built-in multimodal router will automatically parse and explain visual documents.
